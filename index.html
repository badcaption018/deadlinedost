About DeadlineDost

Built for Hack-Tic-Thon · Tech-Melange '26 · Open Innovation

😵 Problem

Students juggle assignments, labs, and exams, and plan by due date instead of effort. Overload shows up only when it's too late.

💡 Solution

A planner that checks feasibility, scores burnout, rewards consistency, and links daily work to a long-term career roadmap.

⚙️ How it works

Earliest-deadline-first scheduling fits tasks into your daily capacity. Burnout = peak daily load plus tasks that can't finish in time.

Tech

HTML, CSS, vanilla JavaScript. Everything runs offline in your browser; your data stays private on your device (localStorage).
